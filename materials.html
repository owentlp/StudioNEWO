MATERIALS

Every material is chosen for the full life of the object.

Lifespan, use and environmental impact decide every material: how long the product will last, how it will be used, and what it takes to make.

Each product uses as few materials as it needs, picked for strength, wear and how they age. Fewer materials make an object simpler to build, repair and take apart, so it lasts longer. The aim is less waste across the lifecycle: fewer replacements, fewer parts, and objects that stay in use.
