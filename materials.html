MATERIALS

Every material is chosen for the full life of the object.

Lifespan, use and environmental impact decide each one. Every product uses as few materials as it needs, which makes it simpler to build, repair and take apart, so it lasts longer.

Any studio product can be made to order in another material from this page, or in another size or finish.
